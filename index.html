Bigogwe — Ibere rya Bigogwe

Nyabihu District · Western Province

Welcome to Bigogwe

This is a very simple page describing Bigogwe — the highland area known for green rolling hills, cattle culture, and the well-known rock called Ibere rya Bigogwe. Use this as a starting point for a small local information page or travel note.

Kinyarwanda

Bigogwe ni akarere ko mu Ntara y'Uburengerazuba (Akarere ka Nyabihu) kazwi n'imisozi itoshye, inka n'umuco wo kwita ku nka. Ibere rya Bigogwe (ibuza) ni ibuye risanzwe rikurura abashyitsi n'abakunzi b'ubwiza bw'akayaga.

English

Bigogwe is an area in Nyabihu known for its green hills, cattle-keeping traditions and the landmark rock called Ibere rya Bigogwe. It is a peaceful spot where local culture and nature meet.

Quick facts

  • Location: Bigogwe, Nyabihu District, Western Province.
  • Highlights: Ibere rya Bigogwe (rock), cattle culture, scenic hikes.
  • Local tips: Ask locally for guided visits — many experiences are community-run.
